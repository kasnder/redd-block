Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Thank you for using Digital Habits: Blocker. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Digital Habits: Blocker does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or usage information to us or any third parties.

The application is designed to operate entirely locally on your device. All data you enter into Digital Habits: Blocker (such as blocklists and settings) remains on your local machine.

2. System Permissions

Digital Habits: Blocker operates entirely locally. It does not collect or transmit your data off your device.

macOS (desktop): Website blocking for Safari, Chrome, Brave, and Edge uses Apple's Automation permission (Apple Events) so Digital Habits: Blocker can redirect blocked tabs — you grant this per browser in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation. Firefox on macOS uses the separate Digital Habits: Focus browser extension. App blocking runs in-process and may show system permission prompts as needed. If you upgrade from Digital Habits: Blocker 1.x, the app may ask for your administrator password once to remove legacy helper-daemon and hosts-file entries; normal use does not modify the hosts file.

Windows (desktop): Website blocking uses the Digital Habits: Focus browser extension and a local native-messaging connection to the Digital Habits: Blocker app (no separate daemon). The app may register extension-related entries in your user profile. App blocking runs in-process.

iOS: Digital Habits: Blocker uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls and ManagedSettings) to block websites and apps you select. You must grant Screen Time authorization in the app.

3. Data Storage

All data created or processed by Digital Habits: Blocker is stored locally on your device. You have full control over this data.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
